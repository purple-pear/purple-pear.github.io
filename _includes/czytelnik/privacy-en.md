# Privacy Policy - Czytelnik App

Your privacy is important to us. Below is information about how the application processes data:

- **No user account:** The application does not require you to create an account or provide personal data (such as name, surname, or e-mail).
- **Advertisements:** The application uses the Google AdMob service to display advertisements. Google may collect and use data, such as device advertising identifiers, for personalizing ads and analyzing their effectiveness. You can limit ad personalization in your device's system settings (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android). AdMob's data collection and retention is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Local data:** The application stores downloaded books, reading history, and settings (e.g., font size) only in your device's memory. This data is never sent to our servers.
- **Network connection:** The application connects to Wolne Lektury servers (wolnelektury.pl) to download the book catalog and text and sound files.
- **Data retention and deletion:** Since all app data is stored locally, it is retained for as long as the app is installed. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.
- **Contact:** If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, contact us at purple.pear.pl@proton.me.
